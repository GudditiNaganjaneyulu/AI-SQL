## Recommended routine

1. **Day 1:** take the practice exam *cold* — no notes. Score it.
2. **Days 2–6:** for each wrong answer, re-read the chapter and redo the matching lab section.
3. **Day 7:** knowledge checks + revision checklist. Anything you can't explain out loud → study it again.
4. **Day 8:** Microsoft's official **free practice assessment** (link on the DP-800 study guide page).
5. **Day 9:** re-take the practice exam; aim for **80%+** before booking.

> The practice questions here are **original study questions** written to the official skills outline. They are not real exam questions.
